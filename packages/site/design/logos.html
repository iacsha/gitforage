gitforage logo concepts

Three hand-built SVG emblems, each at 16, 32, 64 and 512 pixels, on paper and on the night-forest theme. Concept A is wired in as the provisional favicon and header mark. Source files: packages/site/design/logos/.

Light (paper)

A. Acorn

An acorn whose cap carries a git branch that splits and merges back; the stem is HEAD. Provisional favicon and header mark.

B. Mushroom

A mushroom whose spots are joined commits and whose stem holds a branch that rejoins the trunk, with mycelium below.

C. Berry sprig

A forager's sprig: the twig is the main line, side shoots are branches, and the berries are commit nodes.
